Frequently Asked Questions

Still unsure? Here's what students & parents ask us most.

Who are the classes for?
NEB Class 11 & 12 (Science & Management), SEE, plus Medical (CEE) and entrance preparation: IOE, CSIT/BIT, CMAT/KUUMAT, Para-medical & Applied Science.
How do live classes work?
Classes are streamed live by expert teachers on a fixed weekly schedule. Every class is also recorded, so you can rewatch anytime from the app or website.
Are mock tests really free?
Yes — every enrolled student gets access to free chapterwise and full-length mock tests with instant results and detailed solutions.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay and major Nepali bank transfers through a secure, encrypted checkout.
Can I try before I pay?
Yes — book a free demo class or explore our free mock tests before enrolling in a paid batch.
How much are the fees?
Fees vary by course and mode (online vs physical). Class 11 & 12 Science online Rs. 9,000 (3 subjects); physical Rs. 15,000. CEE Medical online Rs. 15,000 / physical Rs. 25,000. IOE Entrance online Rs. 8,000 / physical Rs. 15,000. SEE online Rs. 500 / physical Rs. 800. Entrance tracks (CSIT/BIT, Para-medical, CMAT/KUUMAT) — ask counsellor for current fee. See Courses.
What is the difference between online and physical batches?
Online batches run live on Zoom/app with recordings, notes and online mocks. Physical batches at our Mahendranagar centre use smart classes with the same mentors, plus online backups.
Where are the physical classes held?
Puspalal Chowk, Science Campus Road, Mahendranagar, Nepal. View map.
Is there a demo class?
Yes — free demo class + free counselling. Book on the Demo page. Watch sample demos on our YouTube channel.
How do I track my progress?
Weekly mock tests give instant results, detailed analysis and live rankings. Parents can follow attendance and progress too.
Do you offer refund?
Some batches are marked non-refundable; check your batch terms at enrolment or ask a counsellor before paying.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes — free Android app (Android 7+) on Google Play with live classes, mock tests and progress tracking. iOS coming soon.
Who will teach me?
Subject-specialist mentors — IOE/JEE rankers, MBBS scholars and toppers. See Meet Our Teachers.
How do I contact The Achievers?
Call/WhatsApp 981-0686665 or 976-9884993, email info.theachiever@gmail.com, or use the Contact page form.
